# World Map

## Temple Hall (south end), Temple of Midgaard
Marble temple hall, walls covered in ancient paintings. Large steps lead
down through the grand temple gate to the temple square below. An ATM is
installed in the wall here.
- Exits: n, e, s, w, d
- w: Reading Room
- e: Donation Room (small alcove)
- d: Temple Square

## Temple Square
Marble steps lead up to the temple gate. Fountain here.
- Exits: n, e, s, w
- n: Temple Hall (up)
- w: Clerics' Guild
- e: Grunting Boar Inn
- s: Market Square

## Market Square
"The famous Square of Midgaard." Large peculiar statue in the middle. Roads
lead in every direction; hub of the town.
- Exits: n, e, s, w
- n: Temple Square
- e: Main Street (toward general store / pet shop / out of town)
- s: Common Square
- w: Main Street (toward Armory / Bakery)

## Main Street (east of Market Square)
- Exits: n, e, s, w

- s: Pet Shop
- w: Market Square
- e: Main Street (further east)

## Main Street (east end, edge of town)
